FFmpeg release blocker
----------------------
The current repository contains ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe but does not retain
their distributor, version, configure flags, or license text. Do NOT publish an
installer containing these files until the FFmpeg build provenance and required
notices are recorded.

Whisper/Gemma model weights are not bundled. Model downloads are user initiated.
Codex and Claude CLIs are optional external tools and are not bundled.
